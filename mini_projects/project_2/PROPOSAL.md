## My Project Proposal

**What I'm building:**  
I'm building a Song Lyrics Analyzer in Python to compare vocabulary and themes across two Sabrina Carpenter albums.

**Why I chose this:**  
I listen to Sabrina Carpenter often, and I wanted to see whether her writing style changes over time through vocabulary, repeated themes, and word choice.

**Core features:**  
- Load lyrics from album text files.
- Clean text by lowercasing, removing punctuation, and removing stop words.
- Compute album statistics such as total words, unique words, type-token ratio, and average word length.
- Find the most common and most distinctive words for each album.
- Create charts that show top words and vocabulary richness.

**What I don't know yet:**  
- How to handle repeated chorus lines fairly.
- Which stop words should be removed in lyrics without hiding meaningful patterns.
- The best way to measure lyrical complexity clearly.
- Which visualization tells the clearest story.
